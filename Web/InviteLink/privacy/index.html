Lezgo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Lezgo helps a group of cars stay together on a road trip. To do that, each phone shares a few details with the other cars in the same trip. This page explains what is shared, where it is stored and what you can do about it.

What Lezgo shares with your trip

  • Your profile: the name, car description and avatar you enter in Lezgo.
  • Your location: your precise position, speed and direction while you are in an open trip, so the other cars can see you on the map and get alerts. If you allow “Always” location access, Lezgo keeps sharing it while the trip is open and your screen is off or you use another app. Lezgo stops sharing it when you leave the trip or the trip ends.
  • The trip: the route (start point, checkpoints and destination), the room code and the list of cars in the trip.
  • An app user ID: an identifier that iCloud creates for Lezgo. It tells the app which car is yours. It is not your Apple Account email or name.

Where it is stored

Trip data is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) database for Lezgo. Lezgo has no servers of its own. The app shows your profile and location only to the people in your trip. Your profile and your current trip are also saved on your phone, so the app can reopen them.

Lezgo uses Apple Maps to search places and draw routes, and Siri if you talk to Lezgo through Siri. Apple handles those requests under Apple's Privacy Policy.

What Lezgo does not do

  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or third-party SDKs.
  • Your data is not sold or shared with anyone outside your trip.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Lezgo. Without it, other cars cannot see you on the map.
  • When you leave a trip, or the leader removes you, your car is removed from the trip.
  • Deleting Lezgo removes the data saved on your phone.
  • To ask for any other trip data to be deleted, email us at the address below.

Children

Lezgo is made for drivers and is not directed at children.
