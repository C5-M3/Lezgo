Lezgo Support

Lezgo keeps a group of cars together on a road trip: the lead driver plans the route, and everyone sees the same route and every car on the map.

Get help

Email hello@nathansudiara.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened, and we will get back to you.

Common questions

How do I start a trip?
Plan the route (start point, checkpoints and destination) and open a Trip Room. Share the 6-digit room code or the invite link with the other cars.

How do I join a trip?
Tap the invite link, or open Lezgo and type the 6-digit room code. Only one person per car joins.

The other cars do not see me on the map.
Check that Lezgo can use your location (Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Lezgo) and that your phone has an internet connection. Choose “Always” so the cars still see you when your screen is off.

Lezgo says it cannot reach iCloud.
Lezgo shares trips through iCloud. Sign in to iCloud in Settings, check your internet connection, then try again.

How do I leave or end a trip?
Members tap Exit Trip. The leader taps End Trip, which ends it for everyone; to leave without ending it, hand over the lead first.
